# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lesson hosts create learning games and share them with other hosts. Participants
join live sessions on their own devices. Shared games let visitors inspect a
lesson before hosting it or copying it to their account.

## Product Purpose

Hakoot combines theory, annotated code, and quizzes in guided live lessons.
The interface should make creating, understanding, and running a lesson clear.

## Brand Personality

Playful, direct, approachable. Follow the existing interface and its vocabulary.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, unnecessary controls, and ambiguous editing states.
Viewing a shared lesson should feel familiar to the editor while showing only
rendered content and navigation. Editing controls belong to the user's own copy.

## Design Principles

- Keep lesson content easy to read and navigate.
- Make ownership and the next useful action clear.
- Preserve familiar controls and the existing visual language.
- Support small screens and all existing locales.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic elements, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and
text labels for information otherwise communicated through color.
